About

Systems & Cloud Engineer, from the rack to the tenant

I'm a Systems & Cloud Engineer with over two years of hands-on experience in IT infrastructure. I graduated from Wentworth Institute of Technology with a B.S. in Computer Science, and I've spent my early career turning that foundation into real, reliable systems.

My day-to-day work centers on cloud endpoint and identity management with Microsoft Intune, Azure, and Entra ID, using PowerShell to automate deployments so they're consistent, repeatable, and fast. I'm equally comfortable on the physical side, with networking, patching, configuring, and wiring.

I like owning the whole stack, because the best cloud environments are built on a network that's been done right.
